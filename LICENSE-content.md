the Uke hOOt community over the years. Each post — and any photos, lyrics,
chord charts, recordings, or other media embedded in it — remains the property
of its original author(s) and is presented here as a community archive.

**No collective licence is granted by this repository over the post content.**
If you want to reuse a post or photo, please contact the original contributor.
If you can't reach them, write to [uke.hoot@gmail.com](mailto:uke.hoot@gmail.com)
and we'll try to put you in touch.

An individual post may include a different licence in its frontmatter (e.g.
`license: CC-BY-4.0`). When present, that licence governs that post; in its
absence, default copyright applies and reuse requires the author's permission.

Lyrics, chord charts, tabs, scores, and arrangement notation that appear in
posts remain the property of their original copyright holders. Posts collect,
transcribe, and annotate that material for the purpose of running a community
ukulele jam; no claim of ownership is made over the underlying compositions.
