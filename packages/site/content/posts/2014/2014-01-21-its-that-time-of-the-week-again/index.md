Time to polish up your uke, dust off your songbook and get ready for Uke hOOt at The Kilderkin tomorrow (Wednesday) night. Turn up any time after 7.30pm.

The Kilderkin is at the foot of Edinburgh’s historic Canongate (number 62 to be precise) - part of what tourists call the ‘Royal Mile’.
