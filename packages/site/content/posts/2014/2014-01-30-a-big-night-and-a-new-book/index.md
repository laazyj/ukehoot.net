Uke Hoot will be rocking the Captain’s Bar, South College Street, Edinburgh, from 9pm tomorrow (Friday 31st January). Be there or b L7. 

And, the great project to build our own songbook starts now. You can download the first edition as one document from here:

[https://app.box.com/s/zlawbrxv97u0e30tomfi](https://app.box.com/s/zlawbrxv97u0e30tomfi "Uke Hoot songbook")

Or, if you prefer, you could download each song individually (and the contents page) from here: 

[https://app.box.com/s/gdcwzpq5u11tury5ztxd](https://app.box.com/s/gdcwzpq5u11tury5ztxd)

Keep your songbook in a loose leaf binder, and then when we have new songs, or re-arranged songs, just add them in alphabetical order. Simples.
