August in Edinburgh means the city fills up, the pubs shuffle their
furniture, and our usual Wednesday singalong at the Strathmore takes the
month off. Don't mourn it. We're not sitting still. Both **UkeHoot** and our
[Brothers In Arms](https://open.spotify.com/track/3elV4E1GOKfF0MVRgdp6eU?si=2c870c79d4c945fd) 
[**Uke-O-Ono**](https://uke-o-ono.com/) are out gigging across the town 
throughout the festival. And every show is exactly what it says on the tin: free to get in, 
buckets out for tips on the way.

Here's where to catch us. Turn up, sing along, heckle gently.

## "Free Four": the fine print

Everything here is **free**, so there's no ticket to book. Rock up early if 
you want a seat, drop something in the bucket if you had a good time, and mind 
the neighbours on the way home.

Dates and venues can move at Fringe speed. **[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/UkeHoot)**
gets the updates first, so give it a look the morning of a show before you
set off. Regular Wednesday sessions at the Strathmore Bar resume in
September, same as ever.

And if you really don't understand, shuffle over to [UkeHoot@YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@UkeHoot)
to see clips from previous years.

See you in a sweaty back room somewhere.
