A big Uke Hoot week ahead - Wednesday at the Kilderkin for our weekly singalong and jam session. Beginners and experts equally welcome. And on Saturday we have our monthly strumathon at The Captain’s Bar. It’s a special month for us - four years ago we first convened at the Cuckoo’s Nest in Tollcross.
