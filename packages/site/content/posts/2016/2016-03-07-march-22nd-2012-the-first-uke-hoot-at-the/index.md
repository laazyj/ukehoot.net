March 22nd 2012 - the first Uke Hoot, at The Cuckoo’s Nest. Who’d have thought all those years ago that we would now be headlining at world-famous music venues like Edinburgh’s renowned folk mecca, The Captain’s Bar, like wot we are on Saturday night? Hmmm? The management at The Cuckoo’s Nest certainly didn’t see it coming - they replaced us after a few months with a pool table and a puggy. Ha! Who’s laughing now, eh? Uke Hoot 1, Short-sighted bar management 0. So, if you were there, at Uke Hoot One, leave your comment below.
