Uke Hoot will be celebrating “All Mahalos Eve” on Thursday 31 October at The Captain’s Bar, South College Street, Edinburgh, from 7.30 to 9pm. As well as leading the singing on some vintage ukulele pop, we’ll do some Hallowe'en favourites, and a cheap Mahalo ukulele will be ritually destroyed. Costumes may be worn. If you have an old Mahalo gathering dust under the bed, bring it along.
