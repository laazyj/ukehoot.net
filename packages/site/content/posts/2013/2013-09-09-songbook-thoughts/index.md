The Ukulele Wednesdays Songbook has been something of a success, with most of us having access to it now. Our old songbooks are not quite redundant - most of the songs in the old songbook are in the big Uke Weds book, but there are a few left behind: Jambalaya, Meet Me On The Corner, I Washed My Hands in the Muddy Water etc etc. I’m thinking of gathering up these omissions into one Uke Hoot songbook, with a few additions (Donald - Troosers?) and then putting Harry’s magnificent ukulele lady design on the cover. Any thoughts anyone?
