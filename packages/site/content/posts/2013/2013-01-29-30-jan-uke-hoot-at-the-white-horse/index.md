We’re back again on Wednesday night - a-ukin’ and a-hootin’ at The White Horse, Canongate, Embra. 

And if you are looking for something to celebrate: 

On this very day in 1961, The Shirelles made it to number one in the USA with “Will You Still Love Me Tomorrow?”: 

[http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIP6FSYx0LQ](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIP6FSYx0LQ)

This haunting song was composed by Carole King (and Gerry Goffin) when they were working in the Brill Building in New York - then the centre of “Tin Pan Alley”, home of the music publishing industry. A decade later Carole King was to record her own version on the best selling “Tapestry” album….
