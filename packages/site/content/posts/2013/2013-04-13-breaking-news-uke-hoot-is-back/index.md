After only the briefest hiatus Uke Hoot returns for another Wednesday strumathon, at a new venue - The Kilderkin, 67 Canongate, Edinburgh, (only a stone’s throw from the White Horse) from 7.30 pm on Wednesday 17 April. Thank you to uke maestro Robert Sneddon for organising the transition.
