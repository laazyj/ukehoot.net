Uke Hoot is one year old this Friday! It was last **22nd March** that we gathered for the first time and to mark the occasion we shall be strumming our funky stuff at **The Argyle Bar** in Marchmont (**15 to 17 Argyle Place** to be exact). We shall be welcome to strum and drink in the downstairs bar from **6pm to 9pm** (after that a dj comes on for late night sesh).

Bring your friends. Bring new uke players. Bring your favourite tunes, and bring your ukes (mandolins, banjos, harmonicas, balaikas etc etc also welcome).
