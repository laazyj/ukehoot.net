I am waiting to hear from The Argyle (in Argyle Place, Marchmont) to confirm that they can accommodate us this week - possibly on Wednesday. 

Our “Summer Tour” continues for a few weeks yet - it doesn’t look like any bar will give us a permanent slot until the Festival is over.  Last week’s ukulele flash mob descent on the Pear Tree was fun - followed by a visit to Tricity Vogue’s Ukulele Cabaret at The Laughing Horse. 

Congratulations are due to Uke Hoot’s own Lins McRobie who took to the stage to deliver a performance which brought her the coveted “Uke of Edinburgh Award”.
