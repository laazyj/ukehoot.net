Don’t forget Uke Hoot meets again tomorrow (Wednesday) at The White  
Horse, Canongate, Edinburgh,from 7.30. If you haven’t got songbooks  
yet, scroll down to one of the earlier posts and you’ll find links to  
three of them.

Are there any special occasions to mark? Wednesday is the anniversary  
of Johnny Cash’s passing in 2003 - his reputation seems to grow with  
every year. So perhaps we could give “Fulsom Prison Blues” some extra  
passion in his honour.

Sunday (16 September) is the 35th anniversary of Marc Bolan’s untimely  
passing in a car crash. Ride A White Swan anyone?

AJ McF.
