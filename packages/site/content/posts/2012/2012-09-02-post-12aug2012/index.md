Uke Hoot, Edinburgh’s ukulele jam and singalong is back at the White Horse, Canongate, this **Wednesday**, 5 September, from 7.30 onwards. We are expecting a special guest - Richard Gibson (thanks to Neil for arranging this). Richard is the man behind the Scorpex website - [http://www.scorpexuke.com](http://www.scorpexuke.com)/ - and so is something of a hero in the uke world. Let’s show him a great Edinburgh welcome.
