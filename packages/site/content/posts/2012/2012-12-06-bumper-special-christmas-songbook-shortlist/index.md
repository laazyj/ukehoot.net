Following extensive discussions at Uke Hoot last night, and hours and hours of surfing on t'internet, I now have a shortlist for our Christmas bookie. If you really really want a song which is not on this list, then speak up now. Or make ten copies of your own and bring it on 19 December. 

There are two obvious omissions: The Christmas Song (“Chestnuts roasting on an open fire…”) and “Jingle Bell Rock” - just couldn’t find a decent uke arrangement of either of ‘em. 

So here it is:

Have Yourself a Merry etc

Jingle Bells

Let It Snow

Mary’s Boy Child

Mele Kalikimaka

Merry Christmas Everybody

Come All Ye Faithful

Rockin around the Christmas Tree

Rudolf the RNR

Santa Baby

Santa Claus is Coming to Town

Silent Night

Stop the Cavalry

Twelve Days of Christmas

We Wish You a Merry Christmas

Winter Wonderland

Auld Lang Syne
