Back to The White Horse for another Uke Hoot on Wednesday (from 7.30pm). There are two great birthdays on 19 September. In 1963 Pulp frontman Jarvis Cocker was born - so we [common people](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yuTMWgOduFM) might have at least one song to cover. 

On the same day in 1941, Mama Cass Elliot was born in Baltimore. It was Mama Cass’s voice which gave the Mamas and Papas their distinctive sound, and we could maybe have a go at their uke-friendly hit [Dream a Little Dream Of Me](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ajwnmkEqYpo).
