Uke Hoot is back at The White Horse (Canongate, Edinburgh) this  
Wednesday, 10 October, from 7.30 pm until late.

Remember, if there is anything you want us to play, then bring along  
ten copies of the chord sheet. Ideally, it should fit on one side of  
A4 paper, and be in fairly large (at least 14 point) print.
