A record turn out at The White Horse last night (at least a pb since  
we left the Cuckoo’s Nest). We took up nearly every seat in the back  
room, with only three non-ukers left trapped in a corner (they seemed  
to be enjoying themselves - on a visit to Edinburgh from Albuquerque,  
New Mexico).

The party atmosphere may have been in part due to the appearance of  
our guests, Richard and Wendy Gibson from Adelaide. Richard is the man  
behind the “Scorpex” website which has for most of us being a great  
source for ukulele arrangements for many years now. Richard gave us  
some helpful tips as well as leading us in the many of the songs.

Highlights of the night (for me anyway) were “Psycho Killer” and “Take  
Me Home Country Roads”.

Uke Hoot meets again, next Wednesday, at the White Horse, from 7.30.

AJ McF.
