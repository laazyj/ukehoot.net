It seems the bar staff at The White Horse can’t get enough of us! So  
we’re back on Wednesday to entertain them some more.

It has been another busy weekend at McFlea Towers, putting all the old  
song books in order and binding them together in bundles. We have  
about six sets of the whole shebang (a few sets short of what they  
should be, but enough for our present needs). However, unfortunately,  
sadly and regrettably, I will not be in Edinburgh this week to bring  
them (duty calls in the Big Smoke). So if anyone desperately wants  
them, please email uke.hoot@gmail.com and arrange a pick up from the  
Towers (which are in Shandon in case you live nearby). They’re on top  
of my wardrobe, so Missus McFlea would be able to find them.

Al.
