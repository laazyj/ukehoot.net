Big busy ukulele week for Uke hOOt this week. On Wednesday (8 April) we’re meeting at The Kilderkin (Canongate, Edinburgh) as usual. Then, on Saturday (11 April) we’re bringing the house down at our monthly session at Edinburgh’s premier folk venue, The Captain’s Bar, South College Street. 

Finally, a big night the following Wednesday, when we’re expecting a TV crew to film our weekly fun and frolics.
