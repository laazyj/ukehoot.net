Uke hOOt is moving to the Blue Blazer, Spittal Street, Edinburgh, for the month of August. We’re out of our usual residence at the Kilderkin to make way for Fringe performances, but will be holding our own Fiesta de Ukuleles at the famous Blue Blazer every Wednesday; starting on 5 August at 7.30pm.
