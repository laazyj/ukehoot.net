Uke hOOt, Edinburgh’s ukulele jam session and singalong meets this Wednesday (23 September) at The Kilderkin (65 Canongate), from 7.30pm.  Beginners and experts are equally welcome, as are players of other instruments (provided they publicly recognise the ukulele’s moral and philosophical superiority).
