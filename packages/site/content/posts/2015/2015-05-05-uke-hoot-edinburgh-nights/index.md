But if you can’t stand the waiting, if you are overwhelmed by anticipation, on tenterhooks, and counting every minute until Saturday, then don’t forget our weekly meeting at The Kilderkin (Canongate, Edinburgh) tomorrow (Weds) night from 7.30 pm.
